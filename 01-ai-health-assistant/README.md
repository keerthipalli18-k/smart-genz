# AI Health Assistant

## What is the UI pattern?
An AI conversational interface designed for general health and wellness interactions.

## Where is it used?
Healthcare assistants, wellness platforms, patient education tools, and digital health products.

## Why is it relevant?
Conversational interfaces are increasingly used to make complex information easier to explore.

## Design patterns observed
Chat bubbles, suggested prompts, conversation history, online status, safety notice, and responsive layouts.

## What this implementation adds
A clean health-focused interface with quick prompts, simulated responses, dark mode, and a visible health-safety notice.

## Run
Open `index.html` in a modern browser.
